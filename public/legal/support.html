Support

Updated October 26, 2026. Working name: PShop.

How to try the app

Open PShop. The sample kitchen is labeled Sample kitchen and is not your food. Photograph a shelf, or add ingredients yourself. Confirm any blank quantity before the app treats that food as available. Open a special, add it to My week, then open Groceries.

If recognition does not run

A release build is supposed to contain the vision engine and the model weights. If they are missing, type the foods. The app will not download the model and will not send the photo out for recognition.

Accounts, deletion and billing

Household contains Sign in with Apple, Restore purchases, and Delete account. Deletion removes the PShop account. It does not cancel an Apple subscription. Use Apple ID subscriptions for billing, refunds and cancellation.
