What PShop does

PShop suggests dinners from foods you say you have, estimates additional groceries in whole packs, and keeps a plan and a list together. Catalog prices are US dollar examples until you enter your own. They are not live supermarket prices. The app does not promise that you will save money.

Recipes and nutrition

Recipes are original to this project. Nutrition figures are rough estimates, not laboratory results. Dietary preferences are cooking adaptations, not allergen controls and not medical advice. Do not rely on PShop if you have a food allergy or a diet prescribed for a health condition.

Recognition

Model output is unreliable. Confirm every suggested food. PShop will not present recognition text as a fact.

PShop Plus

The free app includes the local kitchen, the recipe library, planning, the grocery list, export and account deletion. Plus, if offered, is an optional auto-renewable subscription for sync and for cloud backup beyond 30 photos. Price, currency, length and tax come from Apple at purchase. It renews until you cancel in Apple subscriptions at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting your PShop account does not cancel Plus.
