Privacy policy

Updated October 26, 2026. Working name: PShop.

Who this describes

This policy describes the PShop iPhone app and the optional PShop sync service. PShop is a pantry-to-dinner app. It is not a medical device and it is not directed to children.

What stays on your device

Your kitchen, plan, grocery list, prices and settings are stored on the device first, in the app’s local database. You can use that kitchen with no account and with no network. Photos you add can also stay on the device if cloud storage is unavailable.

Optional account and sync

The app creates an anonymous device account so a later sync can be tied to this install. You can link Sign in with Apple. If you do, Apple gives the app an identity token. The server checks that token with Apple and stores the Apple subject identifier with your kitchen. PShop does not receive or store your Apple ID password. ChatGPT or OpenAI account headers are not used.

Photos and recognition

Camera and photo-library access are used only when you choose to add a kitchen photo. The app redraws photos before upload, which drops typical camera location metadata. Ingredient suggestions are produced on the device with Transformers.js and SmolVLM weights that are bundled into a release build. The photo is not sent to a recognition vendor. Suggestions can be wrong. They are not added to your kitchen until you confirm them. The app does not guess freshness or dates.

Purchases

Optional PShop Plus, if you buy it, is an Apple auto-renewable subscription. Apple processes the payment. PShop does not receive your full payment card number. The app asks StoreKit for the current entitlement. A flag set inside the web page cannot unlock Plus.

What we do not do

PShop does not show ads, does not use tracking across other companies’ apps and websites, and does not use App Tracking Transparency. It does not use HealthKit. It does not sell personal information.

Deletion

In Household you can delete the device account. That removes the local kitchen and, when the server can be reached, the cloud kitchen, photos and Apple link for that account. Deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel billing in your Apple ID subscriptions. Apple may keep its own purchase records.

Retention and security

Cloud copies remain until you delete the account or the operator removes the service. Device copies remain until you delete them or remove the app. Tokens are meant to be stored in the iOS Keychain once the secure-storage plugin is included in the signed build. Do not send your kitchen export to people you do not trust.

Contact

A public support page must be hosted by the Account Holder before App Store submission. This in-app page is the same text. The operator has not yet published a monitored mailbox in this repository.
