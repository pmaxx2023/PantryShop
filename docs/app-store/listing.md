## Promotional text (170 characters, editable without a new binary)

Photograph what you have. PShop suggests dinners from that food and lists only what is still missing.

## Description

PShop turns the food you already own into a dinner menu.

Take a photo of a shelf or type what is in the kitchen. Suggestions from the on-device model are not added until you confirm them. The menu then shows dishes you can cook, and the ones that need a small trip to the store.

Plan the week, lock the shopping list, and check off what you buy. Cooking updates the kitchen. Prices you have not entered are not treated as zero, and example prices are not live supermarket prices. The app does not claim you saved money.

Your kitchen stays on the iPhone. An account is optional. You can delete it in the app. Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

PShop is not medical advice and it is not an allergen filter.
